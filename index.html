FAQ

Designed to start small.

Prove value before changing systems or workflows.

Do we need to replace our ERP or CRM?

No. The first audit starts from exports you already have. If a later pilot proves valuable, integrations can be evaluated then.

What data is needed?

For customer-level reorder and decline analysis, we need a customer identifier, transaction date and order/revenue amount. Product-level data improves reorder and cross-sell analysis. Receivables data enables collection prioritization.

Is the opportunity value guaranteed revenue?

No. Reorder, churn-risk and cross-sell amounts are modeled opportunities based on historical behavior. Outstanding receivables are treated separately from potential new revenue.

Who is this best for?

Wholesale distributors with repeat B2B purchasing, a meaningful customer base and a sales team that already works from ERP reports, spreadsheets or account knowledge.
