What Revenue Leak Finder does

Your ERP tells you what happened. Khaacho tells you what to do next.

Revenue Leak Finder is a focused analytics product for repeat-order B2B distributors. It turns existing transaction data into a ranked daily action list for sales and collections teams.

01

Missed reorders

Flag customers that have passed their normal replenishment cycle and estimate the likely reorder opportunity.

02

At-risk accounts

Catch valuable customers whose buying frequency or spend is deteriorating before they quietly disappear.

03

Cross-sell gaps

Surface products similar customers buy that an account is not yet purchasing, with evidence behind the recommendation.

04

Overdue cash

Prioritize receivables by balance, age, customer value and urgency so collectors know where to act first.

What problem does Khaacho solve?

Wholesale distributors often have years of sales history but still rely on reports, spreadsheets and rep memory to decide which accounts deserve attention. Khaacho identifies abnormal buying behavior and turns it into specific actions: who to contact, why they were flagged, and what revenue or receivable is at stake.

Questions distributors ask

Designed to start small.

Prove value before changing systems or workflows.

Do we need to replace our ERP or CRM?

No. The first audit starts from exports you already have. If a later pilot proves valuable, integrations can be evaluated then.

What data is needed?

For customer-level reorder and decline analysis, we need a customer identifier, transaction date and order or revenue amount. Product-level data improves reorder and cross-sell analysis. Receivables data enables collection prioritization.

Is the opportunity value guaranteed revenue?

No. Reorder, churn-risk and cross-sell amounts are modeled opportunities based on historical behavior. Outstanding receivables are treated separately from potential new revenue.

Who is this best for?

Wholesale distributors with repeat B2B purchasing, a meaningful customer base and a sales team that already works from ERP reports, spreadsheets or account knowledge.
